# Tabs

Two strips exist, and a window with one thing open draws neither.

- **Main-region tabs**: one tab per open view of the agent being worked on (its Chat or terminal view, and files opened from the inspector), plus the Board. The strip is drawn **only when two or more tabs exist**. Why: a single tab drew a strip, truncated, over empty width and took height from the conversation for no choice. The strip belongs to nsh's `DockArea`; a single tab is its title in the one header line. Built as `stripsShown(layout)` (`lib/dock.ts`) feeding `tabBarClass` (`hidden` when false). **One group** with one panel: no strip. **Several groups**: every group keeps its strip, even a group of one, so a drop target and the cross stay visible. Each project has its own layout, so the Board (home) and an agent (its project) are never two tabs of one strip: an agent alone in its project draws none. A hidden bar is not in the accessibility tree: there is no `role:tab` then (tests count 0) and the view's own header names it; `mod+w`, `mod+shift+1..9` and the palette work either way.
- **Inspector tabs** (decided, not built yet): Overview, Changes, Files, Terminal. Opening a file from Files adds a tab beside Chat in the main region, not a tab in the inspector. The inspector remembers its open tab and its collapsed state.

The rest of this page is the main-region strip as built. `DockArea` owns the bar, the drag and the selection; `components/tab-face.tsx` (`TabFace`) is what a tab shows, `components/workspace.tsx` sets the bar's and the tab's classes, `ROW.tab` in `lib/layout.ts` is the tab's height (see that file for the number: the type-scale cutover changes it). `TabBar` and `TabItem` of the kit (`design/components.md`) are the filter chips of one record, not this.

## Rules

- **A tab is a chip** (Linear's look, owner decision 2026-11-08): `rounded-md` (8 pt), 28 pt high (`CONTROL.small`), 4 pt gap between chips and 6 pt of padding around the strip, which sits on `background` like the view under it: no rule under the bar, no separators, no underline. At least `min-w-30` (120 pt), at most `max-w-42` (168 pt); tabs of one row share the row equally, the frame clips (`overflow-hidden`) and the title is `Clipped`, one line.
- **What identifies a tab** is the project and the agent: a tab is one working view of one agent, and the owner keeps 10 to 20 open whose titles are long and alike inside a project (`Fix login redirect`, `Fix login redirect again`). So a chip is the project's tile (`Monogram` at 16 pt, the one of the rail and the sidebar, a tint per project name) then the title, then the reserved slot for the cross; the board is its icon, Changes the tile of its project with a pencil. Why a tile and not an icon: Linear's collapsed chips all show the same icon and cannot be told apart; a tile's letters and tint differ by project, the only thing that differs between most tabs at a glance. Two agents of one project look alike when collapsed: the sidebar and the tooltip tell them apart.
- **Signs, by the supervisor's questions** (a tab shows no word, count, program name, age or branch; those stay in the sidebar and the tooltip): needs you is the signal dot on the tile's corner, only while waiting, and it survives collapsing; a finished agent not yet reviewed is a neutral grey dot on a tab that is not in front (existing data: status `done` is unreviewed, `idle` is reviewed); working is a small half-filled glyph, still, never looping. **At most one badge per tile**, precedence needs you, then unreviewed output, then working. A machine that is not online draws the tile dashed. Not built: a failed mark (the board has no failed state: say it when the data exists). The tooltip of every tab says the whole identity, `project · title · state · machine` (the machine only for a remote one) and its `mod+shift+<n>` key for the first nine.
- **What collapses first**: when titles at 120 pt would not fit one row beside the front chip (`tabsCompact`), every inactive tab becomes its tile (28 pt square, `ROW.tabTile`) and the front one stays a 192 pt chip; the order never changes. `DockArea` only wraps (`tab_group.rs:112` gives the bar the tabs as its only children and `dock_area.rs:292` has `tabBarClass` and `tabClass` as its only hooks), so past about 25 tabs at 1280 the tiles wrap to a second row; there is no `N more` popover and no `+` chip, which need a slot in the bar.
- **The front tab is told apart by** a raised `card` surface (one `shadow-sm` ring in light; in dark the shadow is off and a hairline `border-border` is the edge, never both) and the title and icon in `foreground` against `muted-foreground` on a faint `sidebar-accent/40` tint. The 2-pt underline is gone: a surface step and a text step are two signals, and the chip's shape says where the tab is. Weight never changes, so no tab changes width when it is selected. A tab the pointer is over takes `sidebar-accent/70`. The title ends in a fade into the chip's own fill (`FADE` in `components/tab-face.tsx`, one layer per fill: ground, tint, hover, card) instead of an ellipsis.
- - **The cross is always where it will be.** The slot is reserved, so the cross appears in the front tab and in the tab the pointer is over without moving a letter; it is not drawn in other tabs, so a click at the right end of one cannot close it. The cross is not a Tab stop and not read by a screen reader (`mod+w` and the palette close a tab: a control inside a tab would be a control within a control). Not on the board.
- **A title that is cut says all of it** in the bubble of `Clipped` when the pointer rests on it. The tab's name for a screen reader is `<title>, <state>, tab 3 of 8` (`tabLabel`), the whole title always.
- **Right click** on a tab offers `Copy title` and, when the folder is known, `Copy path` (`copyText`, which toasts and announces `Title copied`). A sidebar agent offers `Copy name` and `Copy folder`; a project's name does too and its options menu has them as well.
- **Focus** is the front chip itself: in a tablist selection follows focus, so the raised surface says which tab has it and a chip draws no ring of its own. Why: nsh 0.4.0 maps `focus-visible:` to the focus bit and AppKit focuses a tab on mouse-down, so a ring in the tab class (`FOCUS_RING_INSET` was there) stayed as a dark outline on the clicked tab for good. The front chip's fill must not be overridden by hover (`selected:hover:bg-card`): it showed grey while the pointer was on it.
- **When the tabs do not fit, the bar wraps** to a second row, and a third: every tab stays visible and the front one is never out of sight. A strip that scrolls was not possible: a view in nsh 0.4.0 never scrolls (`overflow-x-hidden` only clips) and the bar is the area's own view. `mod+shift+1`..`9` (`selectNth`) and the Switcher reach any tab by key.
- **Drag** is the area's. Dropping on a bar of several rows picks the slot by the horizontal position only (nsh's `tab_group.rs` sorts the tabs by x), so across rows the slot may be off by a row; `Move tab to split …` in the palette is the exact way.

## Splits

A split is made discoverable, not only draggable (`lib/split.ts`, `openBeside` in `lib/dock.ts`). Why: with one tab the strip is hidden, so there was nothing to drag, and `mod+\` refused a lone tab.

- **Split a lone tab opens its companion beside it** (`companionOf`): a chat gets its Changes (a git folder, as the last read of git said), else the terminal of its pane (`Show terminal`'s view, which a keeper's chat lacks: it then says why); Changes and a terminal get the agent's own chat. Never the same view twice: a companion that is open is shown where it is. The board has no split and says so. A group with several tabs keeps moving the focused tab into a new group (`Move tab to split …`, which still refuses a lone tab).
- **Ways in**: `Open beside` (`mod+\`, the columns button of the chat header, the palette) and `Open below` (`mod+shift+\`); `Open to the side` in an agent row's menu (`S`) and a project's menu (its first agent) opens that agent's chat in a group beside the focused tab. With more than one group already, a new view goes to the next group: never a third column.
- **Back out**: the palette's `Focus the other group` and `Merge splits` (no key: every free chord is a terminal's, Ctrl+Shift+arrows, or a project's `mod+shift+[`); closing a group's last tab collapses the group.
- **`Review changes` opens beside** the chat in front (when the folder is of the project shown) and shows the Changes tab again when it is open: the chat and a waiting question stay in view. In a window under `NARROW_WIDTH` it is a tab of the group as before.
- **Narrow**: the area itself shows one group at a time below 600 pt of its own width (nsh's compact `DockArea`: its bar then lists every panel and selecting one shows its group), with the layout kept. Unverified in a render.
- **Saved** per project with the layout (groups, sizes, front tab): `dock.test.ts` round-trips a chat beside its Changes.

## How it was found

The first bar had tabs `flex-row` with the title's width and no clip: nine long titles overlapped, ran past the right edge and the front tab could be out of sight (`.shots/scenarios.ts` `tabs-many`). A tab's content must be a block of a fixed height in a column frame: `absolute inset-0` makes it zero wide, because the area wraps `renderTab` in a view of its own. Seen on a real GTK window under Xvfb; the headless harness has no layout.
